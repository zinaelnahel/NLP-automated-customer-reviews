### Category overview: positive experiences

The one thing that hits you outright is the build-in marketing Amazon uses in the tablet that you cannot remove. The screen black level is really good and the contrast makes the colors sparkle. Performance is great - fast and easy to navigate. The Kindle Fire tablet comes with several apps pre-installed such as Slik, Amazon's own internet browser, Audible, Good Reads, the Amazon app store, a weather app, etc. You can change the size of the font on the home screen from normal to large or small in the device settings. The device comes with a free 6 month subscription to the Washington Post. Viewing magazine content through the Newstand is a pleasure for the Kindle Fire. The e-ink screen almost feels like a page of a paperback when you swipe, and in my personal preference, I like that. The battery is rated to last 6-8 weeks on average between the various tech sites, which trumps my ipad mini by several life cycles. I despise the keyboard that Amazon pre-installed on this device. There are weaknesses that come with that lower price tag. If you only read for a few seconds here and there, adding an extra device to lug around isn't for you. The Kindle is much sleeker and lighter than the Paperwhite model. The Oasis is about 5" by 5.5" tall at 5" tall. The screen is the first front-lit Kindle I've ever seen where you simply cannot see any trace of the LEDs from any angle. It's the first Kindle I think I could easily fit into a jacket pocket without any effort. The battery on the reader has a lower capacity than the cover's battery, so it runs down faster if you don't have it connected to the case. The power button and charge port are located in the upper top-right corner.

### Category overview: complaints

The Kindle Voyage is nearly 16 months old and worked perfectly. Amazon Voyage dead trying to update software. Amazon does not stand behind this product.
