### Category overview: positive experiences

Amazon doesn't supply a charger when you purchase a new Kindle. This is a replacement model to the original 9W PowerFast charger. It will supply only 1 A output, maximum, not sufficient for Kindle e-reader or other devices. It is a pretty large charger though and doesn't fit in the kindle box with the Kindle at all. Amazon's new PowerFast Adapter Accelerates your charging. charges the kindle quickly. Excellent charging quality with heavy duty cord.

### Category overview: complaints

Overpriced by 4X This is clearly taking advantaged of Kindle customers who lack a technical understanding of what it is. Most buyers will have a phone or other charger that will work so you probably don't need to buy this.
