# Cases and protective covers: customer-review article

> GPU model draft. Check claims against review evidence; use the computed table for ratings and counts.

Consumer reviews of the Kindle lighted leather cover reveal a mix of praise and significant concerns, with performance and durability emerging as key differentiators.

Several users (including one with a 5-star rating) praise the cover’s lightweight design, stylish appearance, and secure form-fit design, which grips the device on all four sides. One reviewer highlights the improved screen lighting, now centered and more even than previous models. The cover is described as stylish and well-made, with genuine leather that has a pleasant new smell and attractive stitching. However, others note the leather is thin and easily scratched, with one user calling it “plasticky” and lacking substance.

A recurring complaint involves structural weakness. Multiple reviews (including one with a 1-star rating) report the cover is flimsy and unsuitable for drop protection. One user states it “will fall down easily” and is not suitable for standing, while another notes it lacks shock absorption. A major safety concern is raised: accidental opening from the back can damage the Kindle’s frame, with one user reporting a crack within a week. The cover’s hook closure is also criticized as difficult to open and close, and some users report it doesn’t align properly, causing the device to wake up and drain the battery.

In contrast, a top-rated profile (mean 3.92) reports satisfaction with the fit, feel, and secure closure, while another (mean 3.5) shows mixed feedback. The most negative reviews (1-star) emphasize poor durability and lack of protection, with one user calling it a “waste of money” and another stating it doesn’t stand or protect the device.

Overall, the cover offers a sleek, lightweight design with secure fit and improved lighting, but falls short in durability, structural protection, and user safety—particularly for users who frequently handle or carry the device.
