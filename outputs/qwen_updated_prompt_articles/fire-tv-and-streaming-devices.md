# Fire TV and streaming devices: customer-review article

**Category Overview**  
This review analysis focuses on streaming devices that function as media hubs for TVs, offering access to on-demand content, apps, and voice control. The devices evaluated are part of Amazon’s Fire TV lineup, with user feedback highlighting performance, app compatibility, usability, and reliability.

---

**Review-Derived Product: Fire TV Stick (General)**  
*Evidence: E21219, E21220, E14730*  
The Fire TV Stick is praised as an excellent solution for users with non-smart TVs or those experiencing poor performance on existing smart TV platforms. It supports major services like Netflix, HBO Go, and Amazon Prime, and enables advanced features such as 5.1 audio decoding and Kodi installation. Users report strong reliability and value, calling it the best streamer for the dollar. Its ability to handle Dolby Digital Plus and support third-party apps like XBMC makes it a versatile choice for home theater enthusiasts.

---

**Review-Derived Product: Fire TV (Refurbished)**  
*Evidence: E21562, E21566, E21569*  
Refurbished versions face significant reliability issues. Multiple users report hardware defects, including failure to boot, screen corruption, and HDMI connectivity problems. One user notes the device would not boot, showing only the Amazon logo and "Unknown Boot" text. Another reports needing to unplug and replug the HDMI cable frequently. These issues suggest quality control flaws in the refurbishment process, making the refurbished version less reliable despite its lower cost.

---

**Key Differences**  
The Fire TV Stick is highlighted for its broad app support and ease of use, while the Fire TV (refurbished) is criticized for hardware instability and connectivity issues. No evidence supports a functional difference in performance or interface between the stick and the full Fire TV model beyond the refurbished version’s reliability concerns.

---

**Conclusion**  
The Fire TV Stick stands out as a reliable, feature-rich streaming device, while refurbished versions of the Fire TV face documented hardware and connectivity issues. Users should prioritize new units for optimal performance.
